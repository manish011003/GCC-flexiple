List of Global Capability Centers (GCC) in India

ByDhan Medhi April 16, 2026May 15, 2026 Last updatedMay 15, 2026

India has officially earned the title of “GCC Capital of the World” — and the
numbers behind that claim are staggering.

According to the NASSCOM GCC Annual Report 2024 and the Zinnov–NASSCOM India GCC Landscape Report, India is home to over 1,800 Global Capability Centers employing nearly 1.9 million professionals and generating $64.6 billion in annual revenue as of FY2024. By 2030, this ecosystem is projected to grow to 2,100–2,200 centers, absorb 2.5–2.8 million
workers, and contribute $99–105 billion to the global economy.

    Why India Dominates the Global GCC Landscape?

    Three decades ago, the question global enterprises asked was “why India?” Today, as the EY GCC Pulse Survey 2025 documents, that question has changed entirely. The new question is “what more can we do from India?”

    The shift reflects a fundamental transformation in what GCCs are for. According to the EY GCC Pulse Survey 2025, 92% of GCC leaders affirm their centers now contribute far beyond cost arbitrage. 87% own end-to-end global processes. 45% participate directly in enterprise-wide decision-making. These are not back-office units. They are the intelligence core of some of the world’s most sophisticated enterprises.

    For professionals and recruiters navigating this ecosystem, knowing where these centers are, what they do, and who they hire is the starting point for every career or talent acquisition decision.

    India’s GCC Geography — Where the Centers Are

    India’s GCC ecosystem is concentrated in six primary hubs, each with distinct
    sectoral specialisations (Source: Zinnov Tier-I City Analysis Report 2025).

    To help you navigate this massive GCC landscape, we have compiled a comprehensive list of Global Capability Centers (GCC) in India, categorized by their primary geographic hubs.

    Bengaluru (The Silicon Valley and Tech HQ)

    Bengaluru possesses the deepest software engineering and R&D ecosystem in India, hosting over 880 individual GCC units. It is the undisputed leader for hyperscalers, core cloud technology, and semiconductor design.

    Here is the complete list of GCCs in Bangalore.

    Hyderabad (The High-Growth Engineering & BFSI Hub)

    Hyderabad is currently the fastest-growing Tier-1 GCC city year-on-year. It has aggressively attracted large-scale banking, pharma, and new media technology centers, offering a highly predictable cost-to-capability environment.

    Pune (The Manufacturing, Automotive & Enterprise Tech Hub)

    Pune is the only city in India with a massive, dedicated domain pool for automotive professionals and heavy engineering. It is the premier destination for manufacturing tech, while also hosting massive banking centers.

    Chennai (The Deep Tech, Auto & SaaS Powerhouse)

    Known for having the lowest attrition rate among Tier-1 cities, Chennai provides incredible workforce stability. It heavily dominates in SaaS, automotive engineering, and industrial design.

    Delhi NCR (The Consulting, Retail & Digital Hub)

    The National Capital Region (Gurgaon and Noida) leads India in entry-level talent demand and hosts massive concentrations of consulting, retail, and digital media capability centers.

    Mumbai (The Undisputed BFSI & Financial Tech Hub)

    Mumbai provides India’s deepest bench for professional financial services. For global investment banks running highly complex quantitative trading and compliance operations, Mumbai is the default choice.

    Emerging Tier-2 Hubs and Other Major Cities

    To combat market saturation and reduce operational costs by up to 30%, multinational companies are increasingly expanding into Tier-2 and emerging micro-markets.

    • Kolkata: Hosts massive consulting and tech delivery centers including ITC Infotech (5,000+), Wipro (3,000+), and TCS (5,000+).
    • Ahmedabad / GIFT City: Emerging rapidly with GCCs like Infineon Technologies (Semiconductors) and DAMAC (Real Estate/PropTech).
    • Coimbatore: Establishing a strong industrial and software presence with centers like Trimble (2,000+ for GIS/Construction tech) and Elgi Equipments.

    The comprehensive list of Global Capability Centers (GCC) in India proves that the country has successfully moved beyond basic process outsourcing. By owning enterprise strategy, driving AI implementation, and building multi-billion dollar products, India’s GCC ecosystem offers unmatched career velocity for professionals and unparalleled innovation capacity for global enterprises.

    FAQ Section

    Q: How many Global Capability Centers are there in India?

    A: As of the 2025-2026 reporting period, India hosts over 1,700 to 1,900 operational GCCs, a number that is projected to grow to over 2,100 by the year 2030.

    Q: Which Indian city has the highest number of GCCs?

    A: Bengaluru is the dominant leader, housing over 880 GCC units and accounting for approximately 36% of all GCC talent in the country. However, Hyderabad is currently the fastest-growing Tier-1 city for new setups.

    Q: Do GCCs hire freshers, or only experienced professionals?

    A: Yes, GCCs actively hire freshers. In fact, 64% of GCCs in India foresee up to a 20% increase in fresher hiring going into 2025/2026, often bypassing traditional recruitment methods in favor of hackathons and specialized internship programs.

    Q: What is the salary difference between IT Service companies and GCCs?

    A: GCCs generally offer a 12% to 20% salary premium over traditional IT service firms for comparable tech roles. They also lead the market in projected annual increments, averaging 10.4% in 2026.

    Q: Which non-metro (Tier-2) cities are becoming popular for GCCs?

    A: Cities like Ahmedabad (GIFT City), Coimbatore, Kochi, and Vadodara are seeing rapid GCC growth. These locations offer lower operational costs, high talent stability, and lower attrition rates compared to Tier-1 hubs.
